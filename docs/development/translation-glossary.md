# Translation Glossary

Unitae speaks the vocabulary of the congregations that use it. This glossary fixes the terms used in
`app/i18n/messages/*.json`, so that every screen says the same thing and translators work from
consistent source text.

The English and French terms follow the official wording on [jw.org](https://www.jw.org) and the
[Watchtower Online Library](https://wol.jw.org). When you add or change a message, use these terms.
When you translate Unitae into a new language, use the official term for your language from the
same sources, and translate from `en.json`.

## Titles for people in charge

Use the real title for the situation: group overseer, Life and Ministry Meeting overseer, territory
servant, attendant. When no title fits, write **person in charge**. Never "responsible(s)", "deputy"
or "leader".

## Typography

- English quotes are “ ”, never « » or straight quotes.
- No space before `:`, `!` or `?` in English.
- Never write "(s)": use a plural message instead.
- French keeps its own typography (« », space before `:`).
