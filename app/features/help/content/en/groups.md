With the right to create groups and appoint their group overseers:

## Creating a group

1. Open **Congregation › Field service groups**, then click **New group**.
2. Give its **Name** and **Address**, where it meets: the board’s field service groups document shows it, with a link to the map.
3. Choose its **Group overseer**, and if there is one, its **Assistant**: an elder or a ministerial servant. Each leads only one group.
4. Add its members, then save.

The overseer and the assistant join the group they lead, if they were in another one.

![A group’s form: its name and address, then its overseer and assistant](capture:group-form)

## Sharing out the publishers

- On a group’s page, **Edit** opens its form: **Add a publisher** brings them in and takes them out of their current group, **Remove** takes them out of the group.
- From **Congregation › Publishers**, tick publishers, then click **Change group** to move them together.

An overseer or assistant stays in the group they lead: to move them, first choose someone else in their place.

The groups list points out publishers with no group: **View the list** shows them.

## Deleting a group

In the group’s form, the **…** menu › **Delete the group…** deletes it. Its publishers are left with no group; their reports stay.
