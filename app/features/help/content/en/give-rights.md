A right is never given straight to a person: it goes through a role. A person has the rights of all their roles.

There are three kinds of roles:

- **Record roles** follow what each person’s record says: elder, ministerial servant, pioneer… They change when the record changes.
- **Congregation roles** are created by the congregation in the organization chart, and given from the chart or the account.
- **Administrator** can do anything, including giving this role.

## Give someone a role

1. In **Settings › Users**, open their account.
2. In **Roles**, tick the role to give them, or untick it to take it away, then click **Save**.

   ![The top of an account’s Roles section: the roles to tick, ticked for those they already have](capture:settings-account-roles)

A role held by one person only (a personal role of the organization chart) is given from the chart, not here.

## Change what a role allows

1. In **Settings › Roles and permissions**, choose the role.
2. Tick the rights it gives, category by category. Some rights need another one: the line says so.
3. Click **Save**.

The change applies to everyone who has that role.

## Good to know

- You can only give the rights you hold yourself; otherwise an administrator does it.
- Only an administrator changes what a record role allows.
- The administrator cannot be restricted: to take a right away from an administrator, remove that role and give them a congregation role.
- The app refuses to leave the congregation without an administrator.
