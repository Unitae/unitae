This page sets the territories of the whole congregation. It has four sections, from top to bottom.

## Building survey

- **BANO file**: the `https` address of the CSV file of your department. Clearing the field stops the sync.
- **Postal codes**: the codes whose addresses are imported.
- **Building survey validity**: how long a survey stays valid, in months, up to 120.

## Assignment duration

Once the given time has passed, an assignment is late. Each kind has its own time: **House-to-house, campus and hotels**, **Businesses** and **Telephone**, in days. A campaign has its own dates.

## Territory kinds

For each kind, the roles that may receive it. With no role chosen, any publisher may receive it. On the **Phone** row, the switch turns the telephone kind on; it is off by default.

## Display

- **Map in My territories**: publishers see the entrances of their territory on a map. This needs a base map on the instance.
- **Accessibility (reduced mobility)**: shows, in lists and on the page of a house-to-house territory, the entrances accessible to people with reduced mobility.
