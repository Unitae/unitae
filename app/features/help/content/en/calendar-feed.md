Your published parts and services, and your absences, can show in your calendar and stay up to date there. You only need to add your calendar link to it.

## Create your link

1. Click your name, at the foot of the sidebar, then **My account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen.
2. On the **Profile** page, scroll down to **Calendar**.
3. Click **Create my link**.

   ![The Calendar section of the Profile page, before the link exists, and its “Create my link” button](capture:account-calendar-create)

4. Click **Copy**.
5. In your calendar app, add a calendar from an address (a subscription), then paste the link.

Under the link, **Read by your calendar**, followed by when, shows that your calendar reads it. Your calendar reads the link again at its own pace: a part you are given can take a while to show there.

## Change or delete the link

Keep the link to yourself: anyone who has it sees your assignments. The **…** menu, beside **Copy**, changes or deletes it.

- **Change link…**, then **Change link**: the old one stops working. Add the new one to your calendar.
- **Delete link…**, then **Delete link**: your calendar receives nothing more. You can create another link later.
