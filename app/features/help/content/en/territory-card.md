The territory card is a PDF to print and give the publisher. It can be downloaded at any time, whether the territory is out or not.

## Download the card

1. In the sidebar, open **Territories**.
2. At the end of the territory’s row, open the **…** menu, then click **Download the PDF**. On the territory’s page, it is the button at the top right.

   ![A territory’s menu, open on Download the PDF](capture:territory-card-menu)

3. Print the file.

## What the card shows

- **The first page**: the number, the type, the number of homes or entrances, then the territory’s entrances.
- **At the foot of that page**: the publisher’s name and the date the territory is due back, when it is out. Otherwise the lines are left to fill in by hand.
- **The map**: a second page places the territory on a map, with the zones and the boundaries drawn in **Settings › Congregation map**. It appears only when the instance has a Google Maps key.

A card that went out for a campaign, or for phone work, carries the word as a watermark.

The publisher who holds the territory downloads the same card from **My territories**.
