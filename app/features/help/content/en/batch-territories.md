**Create in bulk** makes territories one after another from the free entrances: those on no territory of the chosen type. Each time a territory is created, the page clears and offers the next number.

## Create the territories

1. In **Territories**, click **Create in bulk**.
2. Choose the **Type**. The number of free entrances of each type shows beside it.
3. Check the number in **Next**. If it is refused because another territory already has it, click **Take**, followed by the free number offered.

   ![The top of Create in bulk: the type, the next number, the filters and the number of free entrances](capture:territory-batch-header)

4. On the map, click the territory’s free entrances, or search for them by address when there is no map. Click one again to take it out, or **Clear** to start over.
5. Click **Create**, followed by the number. The territory joins the **Created here** list, and the page moves on to the next one.

## Good to know

- The street search and the **Filters** button (postcode, access) narrow the entrances shown, to work one neighbourhood at a time.
- Entrances whose building has no position do not show on the map. The **without a position** link, beside the number of free entrances, lists them so you can add them from there.
- **Create in bulk** only takes free entrances. To take some from an existing territory, see splitting by hand.
- When every entrance of the type is on a territory, new buildings are needed: they are added in the building survey.
