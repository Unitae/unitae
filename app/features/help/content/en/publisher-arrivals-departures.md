## Adding a publisher

With the right to create and edit publishers:

1. Open **Congregation › Publishers**, then click **New publisher**.
2. Give their **First name**, **Last name**, and **Brother or sister**. The rest can wait: contact details, arrival date, **Field service group**, baptism, appointment.
3. Click **Create the record**.

The e-mail address on the record is for contacting them: it does not let them sign in. For them to sign in to Unitae, open their record, then the **…** menu › **Create a login…**: they get an e-mail to choose their password. **Remove the login…** deletes the account and keeps the record.

## Recording a departure

With the right to change publishers’ status, open their record, then the **…** menu:

1. Choose **Left the congregation…**
2. Give the **Departure date**: today, or within the past month.
3. Click **Mark as left**.

![The window that records a publisher’s departure, and what will happen to their record](capture:publisher-departure)

They leave the lists and lose any group responsibility. Their record stays for six months under the **Left** status of the publishers list; it is then anonymised: their name, contact details and emergency contacts are erased, and their reports are kept without a name.

They come back? Open their record and click **Mark as returned**. Before the anonymisation, nothing is lost.

## Inactive

A publisher who no longer preaches stays in the congregation, but no longer appears on board documents or in the congregation’s figures. They become inactive on their own after six reports in a row where they did not preach, and become active again with the next report where they preached. The **…** menu › **Mark as inactive** or **Mark as active** changes the status by hand.

Nobody can change their own status.
