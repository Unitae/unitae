A live document is built from the congregation’s data: it changes by itself when the data changes. There is nothing to upload or replace.

## The kinds of documents

- **Program**: the coming meetings, with their parts or their services. You can add several, for instance one per event type.
- **Field service groups**: each group with its overseer, its assistant and its publishers.
- **Pioneers**: the current pioneers, by type.
- **Organization chart**: the congregation’s responsibilities, post by post.

A kind is offered only when the congregation has that data: groups, pioneers, an organization chart. The groups, the pioneers and the organization chart can be added only once; when one is already on the board, its row says **Already added · Open**.

## Add the document

1. In **Manage the board**, click **Live document**.
2. Click the kind of document.

   ![The choice of kind: program, field service groups, pioneers and organization chart](capture:board-dynamic-types)

3. Give it a **Title** and choose its **Section**.
4. For a program, choose in **Content** the meetings it shows, with their parts or their services, and their order.
5. In **Appearance**, choose the typeface, the accent colour and, depending on the kind, the layout. The preview shows the result, which stays readable printed, in colour or in black and white.
6. Choose when it shows, then click **Add to the board**.

Readers see the document as a page, and download it as a PDF with the same layout.
