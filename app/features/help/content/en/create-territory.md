A territory is made of a number, a kind and the entrances that make it up.

## Create a territory

1. In the sidebar, open **Territories**, then click **New territory**.
2. Choose the kind: **Door to door**, **Businesses**, **Hotels** or **Universities**. The **Phones** kind is offered only when it is turned on.
3. Fill in the **Number**.
4. On the map, click the free entrances to add them to the territory.
5. Click **Create territory**.

To create several at once, the **Create in bulk** button appears if you also have the right to plan territory splits.
