**Settings › Import and export** makes a `.unitae` archive of all the congregation’s data, and takes in an archive made by Unitae, for instance to move from one instance to another.

## Export

1. In **Settings › Import and export**, choose what the archive holds on top of the data: **Include the information board’s documents (PDF)** and **Include the audit log**. Both can make it much heavier.
2. Click **Start the export**. The archive is prepared in the background.

   ![The Export section: the board’s documents, the audit log and Start the export](capture:settings-data-export)

3. When it is ready, click **Download archive**.

Earlier archives stay under **Previous exports**, to download them again.

## Import

1. In **Import**, drop the `.unitae` archive, then click **Read the archive**.
2. The app shows what it holds: the data to import, the items that already exist, updated or skipped, and the warnings.
3. If everything suits you, click **Start the import**.

If the import fails, the congregation’s data is not changed. Importing takes being an administrator.
