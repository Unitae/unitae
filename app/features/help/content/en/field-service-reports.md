Every month, each publisher hands in their report for the month just ended. A publisher says whether they preached and how many Bible studies they conducted; a pioneer also gives their hours.

## From the group page

A group’s overseer and assistant enter their group’s reports, with no other right. Their group opens from **My group** in the menu; with the right to view publishers, from **Field service groups**.

1. The **Report** column shows the month just ended, and the header counts the reports in.
2. On the row of a publisher with no report, click **Enter**. The report opens beside the list.

   ![A publisher’s monthly report, open beside the group’s list](capture:field-service-report-panel)

3. Fill it in:
   - for a publisher, leave **Preached this month** on if they preached, and give their **Bible studies**;
   - for a pioneer, give their **Hours** and **Bible studies**: the month’s goal shows below;
   - a publisher with no pioneer enrolment who took up the service that month: choose **Auxiliary pioneer** under **Pioneer service in …**
4. Click **Save**, or **Save and next** to move on to the next publisher with no report.

The dots in the **Last 6 months** column open their month’s report: a forgotten report can still be entered, and a filed one corrected.

A pioneer spent time at school, on a construction project or on a mission? Write it in **Remarks**: the secretary turns it into a credit of hours.

## From the Activity page

With the right to view activity, **Congregation › Activity** lists the whole congregation’s reports, month by month. It opens on the current month, or on the month just ended while none of this month’s reports has arrived; **Previous month** and **Next month** change the month. The **Report › No report** filter keeps the missing ones: click **Enter** on a row, then save as from the group.

The figures above the list are the ones the branch asks for: the congregation’s total, then the reports of the publishers, auxiliary pioneers and regular pioneers who preached, with their hours and Bible studies. A report counts under the service of its month. Special pioneers and missionaries are in no line, but their Bible studies count for the congregation. Hour credit never counts. Under **Congregation**, the irregular are those who handed in a report without having preached, the inactive set apart.

## Correcting or deleting a report

A filed report can be corrected at any time, whatever the month: open it, change it, then save. The group overseer and assistant correct their group’s.

With the right to edit recorded activity, the secretary can also grant a pioneer **Credit hours**: they count toward the pioneer’s goal, never in exports. Only the secretary can **Delete** a report: the month goes back to « No report », and the report leaves the S-21 record.

After six reports in a row where they did not preach, a publisher becomes inactive. The status lifts with the next report where they preached.
