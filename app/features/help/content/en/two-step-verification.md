With two-step verification, signing in asks, on top of the password, for a 6-digit code shown by an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy…). Install one before you start.

## Turn it on

1. Click your name, at the foot of the sidebar, then **My account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen.
2. Open **Security**.
3. On the **Two-step verification** row, click **Turn on…**.
4. In your authenticator app, add an account by scanning the QR code. Without a camera, type the key shown beside it instead; the copy button copies it.
5. In **Code**, type the 6-digit code the app shows. It is checked as soon as you type the sixth digit.

The row then reads **On since**, followed by the date.

## Sign in

After the password, the **Two-step verification** page asks for the code. Type the one the app shows at that moment, then click **Verify**.

## Turn it off

In **Security**, on the **Two-step verification** row, click **Turn off…**. Type a code from your app, then click **Turn off**. Your password is enough to sign in again.

Changing phones? While you still have the old one, turn verification off, then turn it on again with the new phone.

## You no longer have the app

Ask an administrator of the congregation to reset two-step verification for your account. You then sign in with your password alone, and you can turn it on again.
