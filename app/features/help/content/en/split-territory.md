An area is the entrances of a neighbourhood that are on no territory yet. By hand, you split it with the territory editor, one territory after another. To go faster, **Create in bulk** chains the territories on one page.

## Make the territories one by one

1. In **Territories**, click **New territory**, then fill in the **Number** and the **Type**.
2. On the map, click the free entrances of the area that will make up this territory. Without a map, search for them by address in **Add an entrance**.
3. Click **Create territory**, then start again for the next one.

## Take entrances from an existing territory

To redraw a territory, make another one with some of its entrances:

1. Open **New territory**, or the territory that should receive them, and keep the same **Type**.
2. Click an entrance of the other territory. Its card says which territory it is on, and what that territory will become if you take it.
3. Click **Take from**, followed by the number. Without a map, search for the entrance by address, then click **Take**.

   ![An entrance taken from another territory, and the change that says what becomes of that territory](capture:territory-split-take)

4. Save: the entrances taken leave their territory at that moment.

To remove entrances without giving them away yet, open the territory, click **Edit**, then the entrance and **Remove from the territory**, and save. They become free again.

## Good to know

- An entrance can be on one territory of each type, never on two territories of the same type.
- A territory that is out can lose entrances: the entrance’s card names the publisher who has it. A card already printed still shows them: download it again.
- A territory left with no entrances stays in the list. Delete it if it was never assigned: a territory that was assigned is kept, its history counts for the S-13.
