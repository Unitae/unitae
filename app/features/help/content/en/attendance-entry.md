Each counted meeting gets two numbers, as in the report to the branch: the people **In the hall**, and those **Online**, videoconference and phone together. The total works itself out.

## During the meeting

With the right to enter attendance:

1. As soon as the meeting starts, an **Attendance** card with its name shows at the top of **Home**, and on the **Meetings › Attendance** page.
2. Type the number **In the hall**, the one **Online**, or both.

   ![The Attendance card of a meeting under way: the hall count entered by someone else, the online one still waiting](capture:attendance-entry-card)

3. Click **Save**.

Two people can share the entry: each saves their number, and the card shows who entered what. Until midnight, **Edit** lets you change the numbers you entered; a number entered by someone else stays as it is.

No card? The meeting has not started yet, or it is not counted.

## Correct a past meeting

With the right to correct attendance, open **Meetings › Attendance**. The **To complete** view lists the meetings missing a number: click **Complete**, or a meeting, to see its numbers beside the list, change them, then click **Save**. You can also change a number already entered, at any time. Each correction goes to the audit log.

While one of the two numbers is missing, the meeting does not count in the averages.
