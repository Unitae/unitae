Your parts show as soon as the meeting’s program is published, your services as soon as its services are: each is published on its own. While they are drafts, nothing appears.

## On the home page

On **Home**, **My upcoming assignments** lists your next five parts and services over the coming four weeks: the meeting, its date and, for a part held by two, the other person’s name, so you can prepare it together.

![My upcoming assignments on the home page: three parts and services, each with its date and meeting](capture:programs-my-assignments)

Click a row, or **View the program**, to open the program on the information board.

When you have a part at the next meeting, in the three days before it, **Needs attention** reminds you of it. It also points out a part that falls during an absence you entered: let the person in charge of the program know.

No **My upcoming assignments**? Your account is not linked to a record of the congregation: ask an administrator to link it.

## By e-mail

When you are given a published part or service, an e-mail tells you half an hour later, in case the assignment is cancelled meanwhile. If it is taken from you afterwards, another e-mail says so. You choose these e-mails in **My account**, on the **Notifications** page.

## In your calendar

Your calendar link adds your published parts and services to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook, and keeps them up to date. You create it in **My account**, on the **Profile** page.

## The printed assignment

For a student part, the person who manages the programme can also give you the printed assignment slip (S-89). It shows your name, your assistant’s name, the date and the part’s number.
