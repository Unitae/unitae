Your parts and services show as soon as the meeting’s program is published. While it is a draft, nothing appears.

## On the home page

On **Home**, **My upcoming assignments** lists your next five parts and services over the coming four weeks: the meeting, its date and, for a part held by two, the other person’s name, so you can prepare it together.

![My upcoming assignments on the home page: three parts and services, each with its date and meeting](capture:programs-my-assignments)

Click a row, or **View the program**, to open the program on the information board.

When you have a part at the next meeting, in the three days before it, **Needs attention** reminds you of it. It also points out a part that falls during an absence you entered: let the person in charge of the program know.

No **My upcoming assignments**? Your account is not linked to a record of the congregation: ask an administrator to link it.

## By e-mail

When you are given a part or a service on a published program, an e-mail tells you half an hour later, in case the assignment is cancelled meanwhile. If it is taken from you afterwards, another e-mail says so. You choose these e-mails in **My account**, on the **Notifications** page.

## In your calendar

Your calendar link adds your parts and services in published programs to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook, and keeps them up to date. You create it in **My account**, on the **Profile** page.
