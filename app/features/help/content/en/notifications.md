The app sends you emails at your account’s address. You choose the ones you receive.

1. Click your name, at the foot of the sidebar, then **My account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen.
2. Open **Notifications**. The top of the page shows the address the emails go to.
3. The emails are grouped by subject. Only the ones that can reach you, given your rights in the app, are listed.
4. Turn an email’s switch on or off. It is saved at once.

An email marked **Always sent** cannot be turned off. Sign-in emails (forgotten password, address check) are always sent.
