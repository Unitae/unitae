You can download at any time everything the app keeps about you: your account, your record, your reports, your assignments and your consents.

## Download your data

1. Click your name, at the foot of the sidebar, then **My account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen.
2. Open **Privacy**.
3. In **Your data**, on the **Download my data** row, click **Download**.

   ![The Your data section of the Privacy page and its “Download” button](capture:account-data)


You get a JSON file, a format other software can read.

## Have your data erased

Ask an administrator of the congregation: they can anonymize your record and delete your account.

## Withdraw your consent

In **Consent**, **Withdraw…** withdraws your agreement to the processing of your data. Without it, you can no longer use the app: it asks you straight away to accept it again, or to sign out. Your data stays in place; to have it erased, ask an administrator.
