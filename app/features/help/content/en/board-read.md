A document opens in the viewer, on the same page. A PDF document is marked as read as soon as you open it.

## Read a document

1. In the sidebar, open **Information board**.
2. Choose the document in the list. To see only the ones you have not read yet, choose **Unread** in the **Shown documents** filter.
3. Read it in the viewer. Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit to page** to change the size.

A document whose section is limited to some roles is shown only to those roles. If it does not appear in your list, your account is not one of them.
