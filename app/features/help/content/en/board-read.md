A document opens on its own page, the viewer. A PDF document is marked as read as soon as you open it.

## Read a document

1. In the sidebar, open **Information board**.
2. Choose the document in the list. To see only the ones you have not read yet, choose **Unread** above the list.

   ![The “All” or “Unread” choice, under the title of the information board](capture:board-read-filter)

3. Read it in the viewer, and use its zoom buttons to change the size.

A document whose section is limited to some roles is shown only to those roles. If it does not appear in your list, your account is not one of them.
