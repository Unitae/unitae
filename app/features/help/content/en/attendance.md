Each counted meeting gets two numbers, as in the report to the branch: the people **In the hall**, and those **Online**, videoconference and phone together. The total works itself out.

## Enter attendance during the meeting

With the right to enter attendance:

1. As soon as the meeting starts, an **Attendance** card with its name shows at the top of **Home**, and on the **Meetings › Attendance** page.
2. Type the number **In the hall**, the one **Online**, or both.
3. Click **Save**.

Two people can share the entry: each saves their number, and the card shows who entered what. Until midnight, **Edit** lets you change the numbers you entered; a number entered by someone else stays as it is.

## Correct a past meeting

With the right to correct attendance, open **Meetings › Attendance**. The **To complete** view lists the meetings missing a number: click **Complete**, or a meeting, to see its numbers beside the list, change them, then click **Save**. Each correction goes to the audit log.

While one of the two numbers is missing, the meeting does not count in the averages.

## View the service year

With the right to view attendance, **Meetings › Attendance** shows the service year: the averages of midweek and weekend meetings, the share online, each month’s average and the meetings month by month. The arrows beside the year move to the previous one. **Counted apart** gathers the meetings counted outside the averages, such as the Memorial.

## Get the S-88 card

1. On **Meetings › Attendance**, click **Export**.
2. Choose **S-88 card (PDF)**: the chosen year and the previous one side by side, like the printed card. **Spreadsheet (Excel)** gives each meeting instead, and the year month by month.
3. Choose the **Service year**, then click **Export**.

## Choose which meetings are counted

Whoever manages program templates chooses the counted meetings: in **Settings › Program templates**, open the meeting’s template and, under **Details**, set **Attendance** to **Midweek meeting**, **Weekend meeting**, **Counted separately** (outside the averages and the S-88, for the Memorial) or **Not counted**. Then click **Save**.

On the same page, **Show attendance on the program** lets everyone who sees the program read last week’s total. Averages and history stay restricted.
