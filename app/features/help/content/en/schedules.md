A meeting has its **schedules**: its **program** (the parts, with their speaker and reader) and its **duties** (attendants, sound, platform…). Each has its own block on the meeting’s page, in **Meetings › Programs**: its name, its kind, its person in charge, its status and its buttons.

A schedule’s person in charge is the role its template names, then that role’s holders and deputies. Whoever holds that role fills and publishes the schedule. A schedule with no one in charge is left to those who manage the programs.

## Publish a schedule

Each schedule is published at its own pace: the duties can go out before the program.

1. Open the meeting.
2. In the schedule’s block, click **Publish**.

Its assignees are told; those of the other schedules are not. On the board, members see the published schedules, and nothing of the others.

**Publish all**, at the top of the page, publishes at once every draft schedule you may publish, and says what stays a draft, and why.

A schedule cannot be published:

- while someone assigned is away that day: assign someone else, or remove them. The meeting’s other schedules are published;
- while it is empty: no schedule is required, so one with no part or duty stays a draft.

To take it back, click **Move back to draft** in its block: it leaves the board. The other schedules stay published.

## Add a schedule

1. Click **Add a schedule**, at the top of the page.
2. Choose a template: those the meeting’s kind suggests come first. The schedule arrives with its parts or duties, as a draft.

With the right to manage the programs, **Empty schedule…** makes a schedule for this meeting only: give it a name and its kind, program or duties. To use it again, create a template in **Settings** instead.

A meeting has one schedule of each kind: a program and duties.

## Rename or delete a schedule

The **…** menu of its block offers:

- **Rename for this meeting**: the new name holds for this meeting only, and its template keeps its own;
- **Delete the schedule**: the meeting stays, with its other schedules and its attendance. If the schedule was published, its assignees are told “Assignment cancelled”.

## Edit a schedule

**Edit the schedule**, in its block, opens its parts or duties on their own page: add them, change them, reorder them. Assigning people is done on the meeting’s page.

**Edit the meeting**, at the top of the page, changes its name, its date and its times.

## Change a meeting’s kind

The circuit overseer’s visit replaces the midweek meeting? Choose **Change the kind** in the **…** menu at the top of the page.

1. Choose the new kind: the meeting takes its name.
2. Under **Keep on the meeting**, every schedule stays ticked. Untick the one that must go: it takes its assignments with it, and its assignees are told “Assignment cancelled” if it was published.
3. Tick the schedules the new kind suggests. Its program is available only once the old one is unticked.
4. Click **Change the kind**.

The schedules kept stay with their person in charge. Attendance counts already entered follow the new kind on the S-88 card.
