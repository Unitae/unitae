A meeting has its **schedules**: its **spiritual** schedule (the parts, with their speaker and reader) and its **services** (attendants, audio and video, stage…). Each has its own block on the meeting’s page, in **Meetings › Programs**: its name, its kind, its person in charge, its status and its buttons.

A schedule’s person in charge is the role its template names: its holder and its assistants. Whoever holds that role fills and publishes the schedule. A schedule with no one in charge is left to those who manage the programs.

## Publish a schedule

Each schedule is published at its own pace: the services can go out before the spiritual schedule.

1. Open the meeting.
2. In the schedule’s block, click **Publish**.

The people on that schedule are told; those on the other schedules are not. On the board, members see the published schedules, and nothing of the others.

At the top of the page, **Publish all** publishes at once every draft schedule you may publish, and says what stays a draft, and why. If you publish the schedules you are in charge of, the button reads **Publish your schedule** or **Publish your schedules**.

A schedule cannot be published:

- while someone assigned is away that day: assign someone else, or remove them. The meeting’s other schedules are not held back;
- while it is empty: no schedule is required, so one with no part or service stays a draft.

A meeting created ahead with no program offers **Create the program** instead of publishing it.

To take a schedule back, click **Move back to draft** in its block: it leaves the board. The other schedules stay published.

## Add a schedule

1. Click **Add a schedule**, at the top of the page.
2. Choose a template: those the meeting’s kind suggests come first. The schedule arrives with its parts or services, as a draft.

With the right to manage the programs, **Empty schedule…** makes a schedule for this meeting only: give it a name and its kind, spiritual or services. To use it again, create a template in **Settings** instead.

A meeting has one schedule of each kind: a spiritual schedule and a services schedule.

## Rename or delete a schedule

The **…** menu of its block offers:

- **Rename for this meeting**: the new name holds for this meeting only, and its template keeps its own;
- **Delete the schedule**: the meeting stays, with its other schedules and its attendance. If the schedule was published, the people who had been told of their assignment are told “Assignment cancelled”.

## Edit a schedule

**Edit the schedule**, in its block, opens its parts or services on their own page: add them, change them, reorder them. Assigning people is done on the meeting’s page.

**Edit the meeting**, at the top of the page, changes its name, its date and its times.

## Change a meeting’s kind

A meeting changes its nature, the Memorial instead of the midweek meeting for example? Choose **Change the kind** in the **…** menu at the top of the page.

1. Choose the new kind: the meeting takes its name.
2. Under **Keep on the meeting**, every schedule stays ticked. Untick the one that must go: it takes its assignments with it, and the people told are told “Assignment cancelled” if it was published. A schedule you are not in charge of stays ticked.
3. Tick the schedules the new kind suggests. A suggestion of the same kind as a schedule kept is available only once that one is unticked, and unticking the old one ticks it.
4. Click **Change the kind**.

The schedules kept stay with their person in charge. Attendance counts already entered follow the new kind on the S-88 card, or no longer count if it has none.
