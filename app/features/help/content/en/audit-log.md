The audit log keeps the sensitive actions made in the congregation: sign-ins, rights, data, deletions. Each line says when, who, which action and on what.

## Find an action

1. In **Settings › Audit log**, choose the **Period**: the last 7 or 30 days, the last 3 or 12 months, **All time**, or **Chosen dates**.
2. For a person, type their name in the search. For a kind of action, choose it in **Action**.

   ![The audit log’s bar: the search, the action, the period and the number of actions](capture:settings-audit-toolbar)

3. Click a line to see its detail beside the list.

Actions made by the app itself, such as automatic tasks, show under **The app**.
