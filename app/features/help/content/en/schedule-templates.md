A schedule template gives the meetings' schedules their content: the parts of a spiritual schedule, or the services, in their order, with who may take them. New meetings receive it; schedules already created are not changed.

## Create a template

1. In **Settings › Schedule templates**, click **New template**.
2. Give it a name. Two templates cannot have the same name, whatever the case or accents.
3. Choose its **Schedule type**: **Spiritual** for parts, **Services** for services.
4. Choose its **Person in charge**, or **No person in charge assigned**.
5. Click **Create template**: it opens, empty, ready for its parts or services.

A template no event type uses is added to a meeting with **Add a schedule**, on the meeting's page.

For an event type to use it, open that type in **Settings › Event types**: under **Schedules used**, **Use a template** offers the templates of a kind the type doesn't have yet. A type uses at most one spiritual template and one services template. The arrows change their order, and the cross removes one: its parts and services stay in the template, and meetings already created keep their schedules. Only new meetings follow these changes.

## Its parts and sections

- **Add a part outside any section** adds a part at the end of the template.
- **Add a section** creates a section with its first part. A section without parts is not kept.
- **Add a part to** a section, under it, adds a part to it. Its **…** menu renames it, or deletes it once it is empty.
- Click a part to open it: its name, section, duration, **Where** it takes place, and the roles that may take it.
- To change the order, drag a part or a section; on a phone, tap **Reorder the parts**.

## Rooms

Under **Rooms**, the main room is always there. To add a room, type its name in **Room name** and click **Add**.

In a part, **Where** says where it takes place:

- **The whole congregation**: in the main room, once.
- **At the same time in several rooms**: the same part, with its own speakers in each room ticked.

## Its services

**Add a service** adds a service, with the roles that may take it. Drag a service to change the order; on a phone, tap **Reorder the services**.

## The person in charge

The holder and the deputies of the chosen role can fill in and publish the schedules created from this template, on every meeting. Who holds the role is changed in the organization chart. The name and the person in charge are changed at the top of the template's panel; on a phone, first tap **Edit the name and the person in charge**.

## Duplicate a template

In the template's panel, open the **…** menu and choose **Duplicate**. The copy opens, named “… (copy)”: the same parts, sections, rooms, services and roles that may take them, the same person in charge. No event type uses it yet, and the original template does not change.

## Delete a template

In the template's panel, open the **…** menu and choose **Delete template**. The dialog says what goes and what stays: the event types that use it no longer receive it, and schedules already created keep their name, content and assignments. The deletion is permanent.
