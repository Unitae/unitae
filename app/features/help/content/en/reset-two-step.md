When someone can no longer sign in, the **Security** section of their account offers what helps. Open their account in **Settings › Users**.

![An account’s Security section: password, two-step verification and their data](capture:settings-account-security)

## They lost their phone

If two-step verification is on and they can no longer read their codes:

1. In **Security**, on the **Two-step verification** line, click **Reset…**.
2. Confirm with **Reset**.

They then sign in with their password alone, and can turn it on again from **My account**.

## They forgot their password

On the **Password** line, click **Send a link…**, then **Send the link**. They receive a link to choose a new password; their devices are signed out at once.

They can also do it themselves from the sign-in screen.

## They never got the invitation

Until they have chosen their password, the section offers **Resend the invitation…**. The new link replaces the earlier ones.

An administrator’s account is managed by an administrator only.
