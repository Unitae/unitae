In **Manage the board**, the **Read by** column gives, for each document, how many people read it out of those who see it: for instance “12 of 40”. A document counts as read as soon as someone has opened it.

## See who read it

1. In **Manage the board**, click the number in the **Read by** column, or choose **Reads** in the document’s **…** menu.
2. The **Reads** panel opens beside the list. **Not read yet** and **Read** split the two lists, arranged by field service group.

   ![The Reads panel: who read the document and who has not read it yet, group by group](capture:board-reads-panel)

## What is counted

- Only the people who see the document’s section count, with an active account.
- When a PDF is replaced, or when a live document’s data changes, the document becomes unread again for those who had read it: they move to **Not read yet**, marked “read an earlier version”.
