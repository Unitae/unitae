Record the days when you will not be available. Program overseers see them before they assign a part.

## Add an absence

1. In the sidebar, open **My absences**. You see your current and upcoming absences there; a past absence no longer shows.
2. Click **New absence**, at the top right of the page.

   ![The “New absence” button, at the top right of the page, beside the help](capture:absences-new)

3. Fill in **First day** and **Last day**. For a single day, use the same date in both fields.
4. Click **Add the absence**.

The absence appears in the list, on its date. It is marked **Under way** during the period.

## Assignments during an absence

If an assignment falls on those days, the row says so: the number of assignments, then, for each one, the date and the person to tell. Tell that person: they decide what happens next.

## Delete an absence

Click the delete button on its row, then confirm with **Delete absence**.
