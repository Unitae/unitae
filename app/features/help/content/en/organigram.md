The organization chart shows the congregation’s responsibilities. Each box is a role: a **team**, with overseers, assistants and members, or a **personal role**, held by one person. It starts with the elders and the ministerial servants, kept up to date from the records.

## Create a role

1. In the sidebar, open **Organization chart**, then click **New role**.
2. Give it a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. Choose its **Place**: which role it sits under, or **Outside the organigram** for a role with no box on the chart or the board.
4. Click **Create the role**.

## Name people

1. Click the role’s box: its panel opens.
2. Search for the person. In a team, choose **In charge** or **Assistant**. Then click **Name**. In a personal role that is already held, the button becomes **Replace**: the holder changes, their assistants stay.

   ![A team’s panel: its people in charge, the search to name someone, and its members](capture:organigram-role-panel)

A role can be given to a publisher who has no account. A team’s members are added in the **Teams** tab: a ticked box adds the person, and it is saved straight away.

## Edit or move a role

In the role’s panel, **Edit** changes its name, its type, its description and the **Note on the board**, printed under the role in the board’s organization chart. At the bottom of the panel, **Place in the organigram** puts it under another role, moves it up or down, or takes it off the chart. **Delete the role** is possible only once the roles under it have been moved.

What each role allows in the app is set in **Settings › Roles and permissions**.
