A pioneer is enrolled on their record, for one month or with no end date. Their reports are then compared with their goal, across the service year, from September to August.

## Enrolling a pioneer

With the right to edit publishers:

1. Open the publisher’s record, from **Congregation › Publishers**.
2. In the **Pioneer service** section, click **Enrol**.
3. Choose the **Service**:
   - **Auxiliary pioneer**, for one month: this month or the next, at the congregation’s goal or the reduced 15-hour goal;
   - **Regular pioneer**, **Special pioneer** or **Missionary**, from a **First month**, with no end date: leave **Monthly goal** empty to follow the congregation’s goal, or give the pioneer’s own.
4. Click **Enrol**.

**Continuous auxiliary pioneer** only shows if the congregation offers it, in **Settings › Publishers**.

To end a service, click **Stop the service** and choose its last month: their months of service stay in their history. An enrolment made by mistake is removed with **Delete the enrolment…**, as if it had never existed.

## Following the hours

With the right to view activity, open **Congregation › Activity**, then **Pioneers** at the top of the page.

![The pioneers in figures, above the list of the year’s pioneers](capture:pioneers-summary)

- **Hours to date** compares the year’s hours with those expected so far.
- Each pioneer has a status: **On time**, **At risk** when less than a month behind, **Behind** beyond that. A report still missing ten days after the month ends makes the status one step worse.
- Click a pioneer to see their goal to date and the hours per month they need to finish the year.

The congregation’s hour goals are set from **Pioneer goals**, with the right to set pioneer goals.
