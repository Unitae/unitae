Neighboring congregations send you the list of their speakers now and then, and you send them yours. **Meetings › Visiting speakers** keeps one list per congregation under **Lists received**, and yours under **Our list**.

With the right to view visiting speakers, you see the lists, the speakers and their contact details. Changing them and sending our list takes the right to manage them.

## Update a congregation’s list

On a computer or a tablet:

1. Open **Meetings › Visiting speakers**.
2. Click the congregation’s name, then **Update the list**.

   ![A congregation’s panel: its contacts, the date of its latest list and the “Update the list” button](capture:external-speakers-update)

3. Drop the PDF you received, or click **Choose the file**. Its rows are read and compared with the speakers you have: each is marked **New**, **Changed** (with the value before), **Back**, or **Removed** when the speaker is no longer on the list.
4. Check the rows beside the PDF and correct them if needed. A name already known in another congregation asks whether it is the same brother: **Yes, he changes congregation** or **No, another brother**.
5. Click **Save the list of …** Nothing is saved before: everything applies at once, and the PDF stays on the congregation’s record.

A scanned PDF (an image) cannot be read: type the rows in by hand instead.

A congregation’s first list? Click **New congregation**, then **Create and import its list**.

## Remove a speaker

A speaker no longer on his congregation’s list is removed: open him, then choose **Remove from the list…** in his “…” menu. He is no longer offered in programs; his visits stay, and a talk already planned stays on its program, flagged “No longer on a list”.

Removed speakers are found under **Removed**. Open one to **Put back on the list**, or to **Delete permanently…**

## Send our list

1. Open **Our list**. Its candidates are the members of the organization chart role chosen in **Settings › Visiting speakers**.
2. Open a brother, tick **On our list**, enter the numbers of the outlines he prepares and the contact to share, then click **Save**.
3. Click **Send our list…** Each congregation gets an e-mail at its public talk coordinator’s address, and none sees the others’ addresses. Check the message and the PDF, then send.

Would you rather send it from your own mailbox? Click **Download the PDF**, then **Mark sent…** once it has gone. Under **Sent**, each congregation shows the date it last received the list, and whether the list has changed since.

When you assign a part that allows it, such as the public talk, **Visiting speaker** offers the speakers of these lists, the least recent visitors first.
