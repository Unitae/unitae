A PDF document is added from **Manage the board**. It shows in a section of the board, to the people who see that section.

## Add the PDF

1. In the sidebar, open **Manage the board**, then click **Upload a PDF**.
2. Drop the file, or choose it: a PDF of 20 MB at most. Its name becomes the title, which you can change in **Title**.
3. Choose the **Section** it shows in.
4. In **Display**, choose when it shows: **Now**, **From a date**, or **Not yet** to keep it without showing it. In **Take it off on**, choose the day it goes; left empty, it stays on the board.

   ![The Display section: when the document shows, when it goes, and Featured](capture:board-pdf-display)

5. Turn on **Featured** to put it at the top of the board, above the sections.
6. Click **Upload**.

## If you upload without publishing

If you can upload documents but not publish them, the **Display** section offers no dates: a board manager decides when your document shows. Until then, it is **Not published** in the list. You can still edit the documents you uploaded.

## Replace a document

To update a document, open it with **Edit**, then click **Replace the file**. The old file stays in the history, and those who had read the document see it as unread again.
