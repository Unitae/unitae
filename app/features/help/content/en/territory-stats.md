The **Statistics** page reads from top to bottom: first where the territories stand today, then the analysis of the period you choose. Each block says, under its title, whether the filters change it.

## Today

- **Global status**: the territories in total, available, checked out (overdue ones included) and resting, then the assignments under way by group.

  ![The global status figures: total, available, checked out, overdue and resting territories](capture:territory-stats-global)

- **Congregation territory**: the buildings, homes and phone numbers on record, and the businesses by activity type. A link points to the buildings with no number of homes; it leads to the building survey, if you have access to it.

These blocks do not depend on the filters.

## The analysis of a period

1. In **Period**, choose **This service year**, the previous one, or dates.
2. If you like, narrow it to a **Territory type**, to some kinds of work (door to door, campaign…) or to a **Field service group**. **Reset** removes every filter.

You then read:

- **Full territory coverage**: the share of territories that went out at least once in the period.
- **Assignments per territory**: 1.5 means a territory went out one and a half times on average.
- The average assignment duration, the overdue rate, the average time between a return and the next assignment, and the homes reached.
- The assignments per month, the monthly coverage trend, the coverage by type, the most and least worked territories, and those never worked.

## Comparing the years

The last block compares this service year with the previous one. It keeps the type and group filters, but not the dates.

If you can see the territory assignments, the export button, at the top, downloads the S-13 for the service year, as a PDF or in Excel.
