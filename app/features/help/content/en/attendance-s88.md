**Meetings › Attendance** shows the service year’s attendance, and produces the S-88 card from it. This takes the right to view attendance.

## View the service year

The page gives the averages of midweek and weekend meetings, the share online, each month’s average and the meetings month by month. The arrows beside the year move to the previous one. **Counted apart** gathers the meetings counted outside the averages, such as the Memorial.

A meeting counts in the averages only once both its numbers, in the hall and online, are entered.

## Export the S-88 card

1. On **Meetings › Attendance**, click **Export**.
2. Choose **S-88 card (PDF)**: the chosen year and the previous one side by side, like the printed card. **Spreadsheet (Excel)** gives each meeting instead, and the year month by month.

   ![The Export attendance window: the format, S-88 card (PDF) or Spreadsheet (Excel), and the service year](capture:attendance-export)

3. Choose the **Service year**, then click **Export**.

## Choose which meetings are counted

Whoever manages event types chooses the counted meetings: in **Settings › Event types**, open the meeting’s type and set **Attendance** to **Midweek meeting**, **Weekend meeting**, **Counted separately** (outside the averages and the S-88, for the Memorial) or **Not counted**. Then click **Save**.

On the same page, **Show attendance on the program** lets everyone who sees the program read last week’s total. Averages and history stay restricted.
