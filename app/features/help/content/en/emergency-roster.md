The emergency roster gathers, for each publisher: their contact details, whether they have a **DPA card up to date** and a **Survival backpack ready**, and their **Emergency contacts**. Only the fact that they have a DPA card is recorded: the card itself is never stored.

## Completing the emergency information

With the right to edit emergency information, or as overseer or assistant for your group:

- on the group’s page, a siren after a name points out what is missing: DPA card, survival backpack, emergency contact. Click it to complete;
- or open the publisher’s record: the **Emergency** section has an **Edit** link.

![A publisher’s emergency information: DPA card, survival backpack and emergency contacts](capture:emergency-info)

Turn on what is ready, add their contacts with **Add a contact** (name, relationship, phone), then save.

## Downloading the roster

- For one group: on the group’s page, **Emergency roster (PDF)**. The overseer and assistant download their own group’s.
- For the whole congregation, with the right to view emergency information: on **Congregation › Publishers**, **Emergency roster**.

The roster lists everyone who has not left the congregation, inactive publishers included. It holds personal information: keep it safe, and throw away old copies.
