A territory assignment hands a territory to a publisher until it is due back. When it comes back, you return it: the time it was worked counts for the S-13, then the territory rests.

## Assign a territory

1. In the sidebar, open **Territory assignments**, then click **Assign a territory**.
2. The list shows the **Available** territories, the least recently assigned first. **All** adds the resting ones, which can be assigned too. Type `@` followed by an address to sort them by distance.
3. On the territory’s row, click **Assign**.
4. Choose the **Publisher** and the day the territory was **Assigned on**. The due date follows the period set for the congregation.

   ![The Assign panel: the publisher, the day it went out and the due date](capture:territory-assign-panel)

5. Click **Assign**, followed by the number.

If territory types are kept for some roles, only publishers who have one of them can receive that type. During a campaign, every assignment is made for it, door to door, and a territory still out outside it cannot be assigned.

## Return a territory

1. In **Territory assignments**, click **Return…** at the end of the row, or open the assignment.
2. Give the day it was **Returned on**: not before it went out, and not in the future.
3. Click **Return**, followed by the number.

The territory then rests for 90 days after door-to-door work, 15 days after phone work, or for the rest the campaign set. It is offered again when the rest ends.

## Keep track of assignments

The list puts overdue territories first. Above it, **Overdue**, **Paused** and **No holder** single out what needs your attention: an assignment paused by a campaign follows, when the campaign ends, what the campaign set: resume, stay paused or be returned. **Resume** restarts it straight away. An assignment with **No holder** belongs to someone who left the congregation: give it to another publisher, or return the territory.

To change the publisher, the due date or the notes, open the assignment and click **Save**. **Cancel the assignment…** erases it from the history and from the S-13, as if the territory had never gone out: for a territory that came back, return it instead.
