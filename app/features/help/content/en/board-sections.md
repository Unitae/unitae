The information board is made of sections, in the order **Manage the board** lists them. Each section says who sees it, and its documents show in the order of the list.

## Create a section

1. In **Manage the board**, click **Section**.
2. Give it a **Name**, as it shows on the board.
3. In **Visible to**, choose **Everyone**, or **Some roles** and the roles that see it. The form says how many people will see it.
4. Click **Create the section**.

A section kept for some roles also hides its documents from everyone else.

## Arrange the sections

On a section’s row, **Edit section** changes its name and who sees it. Its **…** menu offers **Move up**, **Move down** and **Delete section**.

![A section’s row, with Edit section and its menu: Move up, Move down and Delete section](capture:board-section-menu)

To delete a section that holds documents, choose to move them to another section, or to delete them with it: their files and history too, with no way back.

## Arrange the documents

- Drag a row to change a document’s place in its section, or use **Move up** and **Move down** in its **…** menu.
- **Move to…** puts it in another section.
- Tick several documents to move or delete them together.

**Make featured** puts a document at the top of the board, above the sections. For a PDF it takes the right to publish PDFs; for a live document, the right to manage live documents.

Creating and arranging sections takes the right to configure sections; arranging documents, the right to organise them.
