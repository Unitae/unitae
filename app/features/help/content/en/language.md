Each person chooses their own language: it applies to the app and to the emails you receive. Other members keep theirs.

## Change the language

1. Click your name, at the foot of the sidebar, then **My account**. On a phone, tap your initials at the top of the screen.
2. Open **Preferences**.
3. On the **Language** row, choose a language.

   ![The Language and Theme rows of the Preferences page](capture:account-preferences)


The page reloads in the language you chose. By default, it is **The congregation’s**: you then follow the congregation’s language.

## Choose the light or dark theme

On the same page, the **Theme** row offers **System**, **Light** and **Dark**. The choice applies to this device only; **System** follows the device’s light or dark setting.
