To sign in, a person needs an account. They receive an email to choose their password, then sign in with their address.

## For a publisher

1. Open their record in **Publishers**.
2. In the **…** menu, under **Login**, choose **Create a login…**.
3. Enter their email address, then click **Create login**.

The account is linked to their record: their name comes from it, and they get the roles of their record.

## For someone without a record

1. In **Settings › Users**, click **New user**.
2. Fill in their **First name**, **Last name** and **Sign-in email**.

   ![The New user window: first name, last name and sign-in email](capture:settings-new-user)

3. Click **Create and send the invitation**.

Their roles are then given on their page.

## Follow the invitation

Until the person has chosen their password, their account shows **Invitation sent** in the list of users. If the email got lost, open their account and click **Resend the invitation…**: the new link replaces the earlier ones.

An address can be used by one account only.
